Blrrdle

About

A daily movie puzzle.

Blrrdle is a small passion project for people who love movies and a good daily challenge. Each day begins with one blurred cinematic frame; identify the film before all three guesses are gone.

How it began

Blrrdle is inspired by the daily-play rhythm of Wordle and Framed, with its own blurry, more challenging spin. It is independently made and is not affiliated with either project.

Frames and attribution

Frames are sourced from Film Grab and are credited on the game. If you own rights to material shown here and have a question or concern, please get in touch.
