Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Effective October 6, 2026

Blrrdle is designed to be a lightweight daily game. We do not require accounts, names, or email addresses to play.

What the game stores

Blrrdle stores your puzzle progress, hint choice, streak, and game statistics in your browser’s local storage. This lets the game remember a puzzle after refreshes and prevents a completed puzzle from being replayed in the same browser.

When you solve a puzzle, an anonymous browser identifier and the number of guesses are sent to our game database. This is used only to create the aggregate correct-guess chart shown in the results screen. It is not tied to your name, email address, or account.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits, page views, referral sources, and site performance. This helps us improve Blrrdle without building individual advertising profiles.

Third-party services

The site uses Cloudflare for hosting and analytics, Supabase for the puzzle service and aggregate results, Google Fonts for typography, and Film Grab as the credited source for film frames. These services may process limited technical information such as IP address or browser details under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can clear local storage in your browser settings to remove stored game progress and local statistics. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at littlebroreece5@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
